# Demo slide outline

1. **Problem:** why carve-out plans are difficult to sequence.
2. **Portfolio:** Nordia Retail scope and the edge cases in the YAML input.
3. **Dependency graph:** shared identity, ERP hub, and inventory/WMS cycle.
4. **Strategy decisions:** ordered rules with an explanation for every asset.
5. **Wave plan:** dependency order, dates, capacity, and TSA/licence conflicts.
6. **Generated documents:** separation plan, one runbook, TSA exit schedule, risk register.
7. **Evaluation:** compare strategies, waves, cycles, risks, and asset references with the expert plan.
8. **Limitations and next steps:** estimate quality, resource scheduling, and optional MCP/UI.
